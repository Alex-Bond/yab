Harbor fees rise as the ferries return

By Ines Carvalho · 6 min read

The first ferries of the season came back to the old harbor on Tuesday, and with them a schedule of fees the port authority had kept quiet about all winter.

Advertisement

Operators say the new charges, which rise with the length of a boat and the hour it docks, will add a few euros to every crossing. The authority says the money pays for dredging the channel, which silted up badly in the storms of February.

Advertisement

Fishermen, who share the quay with the ferries, were not consulted. Several of them stood by the fuel pumps on Tuesday morning with hand-written signs, more puzzled than angry.

The council meets again next week. Until then the old fees apply to anyone who booked before the first of the month, and the authority has promised a page on its site that says who pays what.

On the water the change is easy to miss. The boats are fuller than last year, the queue at the ticket office is as long as ever, and the gulls have found the new café on the pier.
