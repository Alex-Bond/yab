Reader scoring counts real visible paragraphs rather than the name of their surrounding element. This public fixture keeps its original headings, emphasis, list and table while adding independent prose blocks. Each block contains enough ordinary text to exercise the same readable threshold as the current Phone source. The document remains deliberately plain so the structural copy and its original restoration can be examined independently.

A separate paragraph contributes its own words to the article and to each containing element. Links are excluded from that contribution, hidden ancestors are rejected, and surrounding navigation is not treated as prose. This fixture does not need an external article or a remote provider. Its content belongs entirely to the local test and makes the document genuinely readable before any bounded copy operation is requested.

The final independent prose block gives the fixture three substantial neighbors in the same article container. This is not a simulated availability flag: the exact APK integration script measures the real Gecko document. Later cases replace the candidate with similarly readable text before exceeding the character, node or title budget. A refusal must therefore come from the safe copy limit rather than from the readability precheck.
