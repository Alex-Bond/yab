Opens in: choosing a site's engine in Site Controls

Three ways to put the per-site engine choice (Recommended, Chromium, WebKit) into today's Site Controls panel. The panel is redrawn from SiteControls.swift: 280 pt wide, inset 8, popover corner 20, rows of a 28 pt round icon with 12.5/11.5 pt words, the app's own glyphs and palette. Shown on netflix.com, which is on the DRM list, so Recommended means WebKit there. Only the build's model and bench verb exist; none of this UI is built.

Today, drawn by the app

Bench site-shot of the current panel, light and dark, for comparison.

Light

AA row in "This site" with a menu

An Opens in row always sits in This site on web pages, under Block ads and Dark page. Its second line says the engine in use. Pressing the row opens a native menu (AppKit, like PictureMenu) with the three choices; a chosen engine fills the row’s round icon, as other rows do while they are on.

Most findable; costs one more row on every page, for a setting most people never touch.

BQuiet until changed: in "…", with a row only when it matters

Nothing new on the panel by default. The … menu at the foot gets Opens in › with the three choices. A row appears in This site only when the engine differs from what the window would use: a choice made here, or Yab having moved the tab to WebKit for protected video. This follows the panel’s rule of showing only what there is something to say about.

Keeps the panel as calm as today; the choice is one level deeper, but the row shows up exactly when someone wonders why.

CA row with the three choices in a track

The Opens in row carries the three choices directly, in the sliding glass track Settings already uses (GlassTrack, Theme and the agent choosers). One press, no menu; the row is two lines taller.

Fastest to change, but the tallest and busiest; "Recommended" barely fits three across 280 points.
