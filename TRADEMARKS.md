The marks are "Yab", "Yet Another Browser", the Yab logo, the app icons and
their variants (the seasonal, unlockable and alternate icons), and the
yetanotherbrowser.com name.

## What you may do

- Say that your work is based on Yab, or works with Yab, in plain words
  ("a fork of Yab", "a plugin for Yab").
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, reviews and
  package listings that point to the official source.
- Build and run Yab from this repository for yourself, unchanged or changed,
  under the name `Yab Dev`, which `./build.sh` already uses.

## What needs a different name

- Distributing a modified build, a fork or a derived app: give it its own
  name and its own icon, and change the bundle identifier
  (`dev.serafim.fast`, `dev.serafim.fast.iphone`) and the update feed so it
  never updates from or to the official Yab.
- Anything that could make people think it is the official Yab or endorsed
  by it: names that contain "Yab", look-alike icons, domains or store
  listings.
