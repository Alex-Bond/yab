Onboarding v3 spec

Owner: Mira Lindqvist · Review: today with Jonas, Anna and Elliot · Ships to 10% on Thursday

Today half of the people who sign up never connect a calendar, and without a calendar Tern can't do anything for them. Onboarding v3 gets the calendar connected in the first minute and leaves everything else for later.

Goals

  • Connect a calendar in the first minute, on the first screen.
  • Raise activation, a first booking within 7 days, from 38% to 45% by the end of Q4.
  • Measure time to first booking, not sign-up completion.

The flow

One welcome screen instead of four. The calendar is connected in the first minute, the team comes later.

  1. Welcome. Name, work email, and one button to connect a Google or Microsoft calendar.
  2. Your week. Tern shows this week with its focus blocks already found, so the value is there right away.
  3. First booking page. A link to share, with working hours taken from the calendar.
  4. Invite the team. From inside the product, once there is something worth sharing.

What we drop

  • The role question. We guess it from the calendar instead.
  • The product tour. Empty states teach in place.
  • The time zone picker. The calendar already knows.

Empty states

Teams with no calendars connected see one card that says what Tern needs and why, with the connect button right on it. Kai is drawing the illustrations for the welcome screen and the empty states.

Open questions

  • Do we ask for focus hours on day one, or learn them from the calendar first?
  • What does the error say when Google says no?
