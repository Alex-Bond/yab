Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Write to us, a person replies within a day.

How does Tern decide which time is best?

Tern reads everyone's working hours and focus blocks, then scores every open slot by how much focus it would cost. The slot that moves the least focus time, for the fewest people, comes first. Nothing moves until you say so.

What counts as a seat?

Anyone on your team whose calendar Tern looks after. Guests you invite to meetings, and people who only book time through your links, never need a seat.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes, whenever you like, from Settings › Billing. Moving to yearly takes effect right away, and what's left of your current month is credited.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and schools get 50% off Team and Business. Write to us from your organization's address and we'll set it up the same day.

Where is my calendar data stored?

In the EU, in Frankfurt and Stockholm. Tern keeps only what it needs to find times, never stores event descriptions, and removes everything when you leave.

What changes when Tern 2.0 launches on October 21?

Everyone on the early list gets access that morning. Paid plans move over on their own, at the same price, and your focus blocks come with you.
