# Snippets everywhere

Built 2026-10-13 (branch `snippets`). Snippets stop being Mail's alone: one list for all of Yab, dropped into any field with `;name` or found with ⌘;, and Yab offers to keep text you paste again and again.

Concept, every state clickable: [artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/SgkAaUaLwpgLEa7ofGik1X), also kept here as [snippets/concept.html](snippets/concept.html).

## Where it lives

- `MailSnippets` (`MailSnippets.swift`) is the one list: name, words, where it works (`mailOnly`), its key (`hotkey`, as `Hotkey` stores one), Subject, To, Cc, Bcc and files (Mail only), how often and when last used. A snippet an older Yab kept with To, Cc, Bcc, a subject or files reads back as Mail only; the rest as Everywhere.
- `Snippets.swift`: `PageSnippets` (`;name` in a page's box, the list under the caret, a snippet's key), `SnippetOffers` (the paste count and its card), `SnippetSettings` (the two switches).
- `Resources/PageScripts/snippets.js`, loaded after `forms.js` on the same `yabForms` channel: reports `;name` at the caret with the caret's line, puts a snippet's words over it (`__yabSnippetPut`, through `execCommand('insertText')` so the page's undo takes it back, its first `{{place}}` chosen), and reports pastes into boxes that take words. It never looks at password boxes, `one-time-code` boxes, or anything inside CodeMirror, Monaco, Ace, `pre` or `code`.
- ⌘; outside a mail is the field's Snippets place (`FieldPlace.snippets`, `Keymap` `.snippets`): Return puts the snippet in the box the caret was in, ⌘↩ copies its words, ⌘E and ⌘N write one in place (`PlaceEdit.Kind.snippet`, the words field takes ⌥↩ for a new line), ⌃X deletes. In a mail ⌘; is still Mail's field (`.mailSnippet`).
- Settings › Snippets (`SnippetsPage` in `MailSnippetSettings.swift`) sits beside Mail. New Snippet… and a snippet clicked open the editor as a card over the pane (`SnippetCardLayer`), laid out as the concept's Create Snippet: words and their placeholders on the left, name after ;, hotkey and Everywhere or In Mail only on the right, Save ⌘↩ at the foot. Not a sheet: AppKit's slide and dimming took 350 ms (620 cold), the card is up in about 25 ms (`evals/snippets/editor.py`). Snippets with keys are listed in Settings › Shortcuts as their own group. `Keymap.clash` refuses a snippet's key to any other shortcut; a snippet refuses a key without ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, or one a Selection Command has, and takes a browser action's key on a second press.
- **Bring In** (`SnippetSources.swift`, `SnippetBringIn.swift`) takes snippets from other apps. Beside New Snippet… a Bring In menu lists what this Mac has, with counts: Text Replacements (a copy of `~/Library/KeyboardServices/TextReplacements.db`), Alfred (the JSON files in its preferences' `snippets`, or the synced folder its `prefs.json` names) and espanso (its `match/*.yml`). Raycast… and From a File… open a panel: Raycast's own store is encrypted, so its snippets come from the JSON its Export Snippets saves (`[{name, text, keyword, tags}]`); a file is told apart by what is in it (Raycast's JSON, one of Alfred's files, espanso's YAML, a TextExpander group as CSV). None of these places is behind a macOS permission, and nothing scans Downloads. While the list is empty the same sources stand in it, each with Bring In (Raycast with Choose File…). A card over the pane (`SnippetBringCard`) shows each one ticked with its `;name` and words: a keyword's marks go (";sig", "!sig" → `sig`, as `snippets.js` takes letters, digits, `_` and `-`), `{cursor}`, an `{argument}`, espanso's `$|$` and TextExpander's `%|` become a `{{place}}`, `{clipboard}` and a plain `{date}` stay Yab's, and anything else (`{selection}`, `{time}`, a formatted date, TextExpander fill-ins) stays as written with an orange note. One already here word for word is unticked ("Already here"); a taken name gets a number (`MailSnippets.planned`). Bring In ⌘↩ writes them once (`bringIn`) and lights them in the list for a moment. Superhuman keeps its snippets on its servers with no export, so an agent reads them (`SnippetSuperhuman`): offered with an agent connected to someone who has Superhuman's app or its web app in their history, it is a sealed browser run that opens mail.superhuman.com in Yab, signed in as the user is there, reads Settings › Snippets without changing anything and answers JSON; a card says it is reading (Stop ends the run), then the same review card. Superhuman's `{first_name}` stays, a full name is `{name}`, the sender's own `{my_name}`; one with To, Cc, Bcc or a subject comes in as In Mail only. A sign-in page it can't pass, or a plan out of room, ends it with a notice in the agent's words. Checked by `SnippetSourcesTests`, `evals/snippets/bring_in.py` and `evals/snippets/superhuman.py` (a real agent against a stand-in for Superhuman's settings, `CLI=codex` when Claude's plan is out of room); not against a real Superhuman account.
- The paste count keeps SHA-256 digests and dates in `snippet-pastes.json`, never the words, and nothing from a private tab or a password manager's copy.

## The plan, as built

1. **One list for all of Yab.** `MailSnippets` becomes the general list. Each snippet says where it works: Everywhere, or In Mail only. Subject, To, Cc, Bcc and files stay, on Mail snippets only.
2. **⌘; on any page and in mail** opens the snippets panel: search, the list with each `;name`, the words and what is known about the snippet on the right. Return drops it in where the caret was, ⌘N makes a new one, ⌘E edits the one picked. Making and editing happen in the same panel.
3. **`;name` in any field on a page** hangs a list under the field, drawn as autofill's lists are (`DropFrame`), with no hint row under it. Return or Tab drops it in, Escape closes it. Never in password fields or code editors; Mail snippets don't show there.
4. **A hotkey per snippet**, if wanted, recorded by the same recorder as Shortcuts and Selection Commands and checked by the same rules in `Keymap`: a key the browser has is taken by pressing it again, a key a command or another snippet has is refused, a key with no ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥ is refused. Snippets with hotkeys show in Shortcuts as a Snippets group.
5. **Placeholders**: `{date}`, `{clipboard}` and `{{place}}` everywhere; in mail also `{first_name}`, `{name}` and `{my_name}`.
6. **Offer to keep what you paste often.** The same text pasted 50 times in 7 days brings the card that asks over a page, the way Save password does (`KeepCard`, `CardFrame`): "Save as a snippet?", "You pasted this 50 times this week. Next time, type ;linkedin.", the text, Not Now (esc) and Save (Return). A click beside the card is Not Now, and that text isn't offered again for 30 days. The name comes ready; it changes in Settings. Never counted: passwords, one-time codes, pastes into password fields, text under 12 characters. The count keeps only hashes, on this Mac.
7. **Settings › Snippets** is a page of its own beside Mail: the list with where each works and its hotkey, New Snippet…, and switches for `;` in fields and the paste offer. The Snippets row leaves Settings › Mail.

## Not in this plan

Snippets never call a model: anything with a prompt is a Selection Command ([command-builder.md](command-builder.md)). No keywords other than `;name`, no tags, no `;` in ⌘T or ⌘J.
